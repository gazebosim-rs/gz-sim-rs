## Adaptive placement in changing contact scenes

In the off-cadence landing experiment, a stable load allows a longer interval between measurements. A change in the set of contacting bodies shortens it again. Across 60 matched runs in each of four cells, the observed p95 time to a fresh sample fell from 17 to 9 ticks. There were also 54 changes per cell whose next measurement fell outside the recording. In an already split scene, querying only resident owners shortened the median probe-opening barrier from 974 to 921 µs on adjacent IDs and 765 to 730 µs on separated IDs. Physical steps still fence all participants. [Sampling policy, censored events and complete paired timings](../artifacts/multifidelity-periodic-placement/README.md).

The new pool and normal SDF APIs record or replay the optional whole-island choice for each advancing tick. Replay checks current membership, ownership generations, inputs and required contact routing. Both 350-tick layouts reproduced native state and owner generations with zero timing probes. The recorded raw trajectories included seven moves each. The public Bevy paths handled 24 queued wrenches and three saved cuts. A separate 1,000,100-tick recurrent contact run completed 1,251 automatic moves, 2,500 prescribed warm moves, 500 actual worker deaths, 100 disk restores and 10,000 landings. Its complete physics digest matched the uninterrupted native reference. The largest vertical impulse balance residual was 2.665e−14 N·s. [Replay contract and interrupted barriers](../artifacts/multifidelity-placement-replay/README.md) · [Million-tick physical references](../artifacts/multifidelity-recurrent-contact/README.md) · [Late automatic handoff](../artifacts/multifidelity-periodic-placement/recurrent-auto-encounter.md).

All 64 finite-table scenarios matched the independent native solver on every barrier. Across 768 archived observations taken before or after faults, the final native point impulses, both COM levers and the normal matched the independent native world.
