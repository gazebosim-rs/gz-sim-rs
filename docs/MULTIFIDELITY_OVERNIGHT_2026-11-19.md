## Correctness findings

A frozen two-worker campaign found accepted impulses could pin a free incoming body just before a newly added force required migration into a loaded solver. Replaying seeds 20261514821 and 20261521923 reproduced the old refusals at ticks 86 and 123. The planner now transports the complete cold native solver with its ordered input ledger on this route. Both seeds then completed 216/216 exact frames, two positive contacts and a worker replacement. [Permanent regression](../crates/gz-physics-distributed/tests/field_arrivals.rs). [Corrected seed traces](../artifacts/multifidelity-field-sweep/corrected).

In large stacks a monolithic Rapier world and separated native worlds can produce different trajectories on identical inputs. Introducing the 125th two-box stack can change the monolithic constraint order. At 256 boxes the largest position difference was 75.94 mm at 5 ms, 47.97 mm at 2 ms and 35.97 mm at 1 ms. Partition-matched controls agree. Increasing the number of inner solver sweeps reduced the 5 ms peak to 2.99 mm in one tested configuration, with different behavior at 2 ms. A distinct reporting bug added stale impulses from inactive contact points. The corrected active-row result of 19.619996808 N agrees with the independently inferred 19.619996817 N, versus 25.340291011 N previously. Dynamics and genuine warm-start caches are preserved. [Stack mechanics and raw controls](../artifacts/multifidelity-stack-mechanics).

A matched study of 384 oblique sphere pairs at each of four steps compared distributed frames, analytical first contact and smooth-sphere impact mechanics. All 214,272 native frame comparisons were exact. The frictionless outgoing velocity p95 error fell from 0.05587 m/s at 10 ms to 0.01063 m/s at 1 ms. The angular-momentum residual did not decrease monotonically across all four public steps. [Measurements, figure and reproduction](../artifacts/multifidelity-sphere-refinement/README.md).
